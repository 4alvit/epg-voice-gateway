# Code of Conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Welcome differing experience and viewpoints; give technical feedback about the work, accept corrections, and protect other people's privacy. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing and persistent unwanted contact are prohibited in this project's repositories and community interactions.

Report conduct concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and context; avoid publishing private information. The maintainer handles reports confidentially where possible, acknowledges them and explains next steps. Depending on severity, responses can include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions or exclusion from project spaces. Participants may request reconsideration through the same contact. There is no independent moderator or appeal panel at present; a report concerning the maintainer may therefore require the platform's own reporting process.

Security vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md, not public conduct discussions.
